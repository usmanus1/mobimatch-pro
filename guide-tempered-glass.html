Waterdrop Cut vs. No-Cut Tempered Glass: Complete 2026 Shopkeeper Guide

Updated: November 2026 • By MobiMatch Pro Hardware Bench Team • 5 min read

Stocking individual tempered glass protectors for every new smartphone model is expensive and wastes shelf space. By understanding the difference between Waterdrop Cut and No-Cut (Without-Hole) templates, mobile repair shops can cover nearly 80% of daily walk-in customers with just 5 master glass SKUs.

1. What is Waterdrop Cut (V-Notch / U-Notch) Tempered Glass?

Waterdrop Cut protectors (such as SG026 or Mietubl MTG0073) have a black silk-screen U or V-shaped border printed around the top selfie camera. They are designed strictly for V-Notch screens such as Samsung Galaxy A12, A02s, A03s, A04, Vivo Y20, Y21, Realme C11, C20, C21, OPPO A15, A16, and Redmi 9A/9C.

⚠️ Critical Mistake: Never install a Waterdrop Cut glass onto a Center Punch-Hole phone. The black V-notch border will sit directly over the selfie camera lens and cause blurry photos.

2. Why No-Cut (Without-Hole / Clear Top) Glass Saves Inventory

No-Cut protectors (such as SG065 and SG069) feature completely transparent glass across the top camera area with no black camera ring. Because the glass is clear, a single 6.72-inch No-Cut glass cleanly fits both Center Punch-Hole and V-Notch displays—covering Realme C55, C65, C67, C75, OPPO A58, A60, A3x, Vivo T3x, T4x, Y28 4G, Y29, and OnePlus Nord CE3 Lite.

3. Scope Lock: Tempered Glass vs. OCA Sheet vs. Outer Refurb Glass

  • Tempered Glass Protector: External stick-on accessory. Sharing a tempered glass cut never means the LCD Combo or Touch Digitizer is interchangeable.
  • Outer Refurbishment Glass: The bare top glass lens laminated onto an LCD/OLED during glass-only refurbishment.
  • OCA Adhesive Sheet: Double-sided optical clear adhesive film (use 200µm for AMOLED/in-display fingerprint screens and 250µm Mitsubishi OCA for IPS LCDs).

4. Pre-Installation Dry-Fit Checklist

Before peeling the adhesive backing, always place the glass over the screen to verify: (1) Height × Width millimeter alignment, (2) Corner radius curve, (3) Proximity sensor clearance, and (4) Case-friendly edge gap so the customer's back cover does not lift the glass.
