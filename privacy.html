Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Local In-Browser Data Storage (100% Client-Side Privacy)

MobiMatch Pro is architected as a privacy-first progressive web application. Your custom part matches, saved favorites, shop drawer stock counts, donor-phone inventory, and crash/panic logs pasted into our Local Log Analyzer are processed and stored locally within your browser's localStorage. We do not transmit customer device IMEIs, serial numbers, or diagnostic crash logs to external servers.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-6392471291906782) to serve advertisements when you visit www.mobimatchpro.com:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to MobiMatch Pro and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

3. Camera & Microphone Permissions

When you use the optional Voice Search or Photo Stencil/Barcode Scanner features, your browser requests temporary access to your microphone or camera. Video and image frames are processed locally on your device solely to read part codes and are never recorded or uploaded to any server.

4. Technical Disclaimer & Authorized Service Scope

All hardware compatibility data, multimeter readings, and factory recovery procedures are provided as an informational reference for trained technicians. Always disconnect the battery prior to motherboard work and verify customer ownership before performing any factory reset.
