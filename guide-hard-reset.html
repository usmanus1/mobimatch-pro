🔄 RECOVERY & HARD RESET GUIDE

Master Smartphone Hard Reset & Recovery Mode Guide (2026 Edition)

Updated: November 2026 • By MobiMatch Pro Hardware Bench Team • 5 min read

Entering Android Recovery Mode or iOS Recovery/DFU mode has changed significantly across newer Android 11–16 and iOS 15–18 updates. Below are the verified button combinations and hardware requirements for major smartphone brands.

🛡️ Ownership & Data Notice: Factory resetting erases all user data. Always ensure the device owner has backed up their files and signed out of Google, Samsung, Mi, or Apple accounts before resetting.

1. Samsung Galaxy (Android 11 to 16 / One UI 3.0–7.0)

Modern Samsung Galaxy A-series (A14, A15, A16, A25, A35, A55, A56) and S-series (S22–S26) require an active USB-C data connection to a PC (or another phone via OTG) to enter Recovery Mode. A wall charger alone will not work.

  1. Connect the phone to a computer using a USB data cable.
  2. Hold Volume Down + Power for 7 seconds until the screen goes completely black.
  3. The instant the screen turns black, immediately switch to holding Volume Up + Power.
  4. Release Power when the Samsung logo appears while keeping Volume Up held until Android Recovery opens.
  5. Select Wipe data/factory reset → Factory data reset → Reboot system now.

2. Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI & HyperOS)

Power off the phone completely, then hold Volume Up + Power until the Mi/Redmi/POCO logo appears. Release Power while holding Volume Up to enter Mi Recovery, then select Wipe Data → Wipe All Data → Confirm. (Use Volume Down + Power for Fastboot Mode.)

3. OPPO / Realme / OnePlus (ColorOS & Realme UI)

Power off the device and hold Volume Down + Power. Select English → Format Data. Type the 4-digit verification code shown on screen and tap Format. Note: Unisoc models (Realme C30, C33, C51, C53, Note 50) use standard Android Recovery—at the 'No Command' robot screen, hold Power and tap Volume Up once.

4. Infinix / Tecno / itel ('No Command' Trick)

Hold Volume Up + Power until the fallen Android robot ('No Command') appears. Hold down the Power button first, tap Volume Up once, and release both to reveal the Recovery menu.

5. Apple iPhone 8 to iPhone 17 Pro Max (Recovery Mode)

Connect the iPhone to a PC with iTunes/Finder. Quickly press and release Volume Up, quickly press and release Volume Down, then press and hold the Side (Power) button continuously—do not release at the Apple logo—until the 'Connect to Computer' cable icon appears.
