About MobiMatch Pro — Mobile Hardware Cross-Reference & Bench Diagnostic Platform

MobiMatch Pro (www.mobimatchpro.com) is a specialized bilingual (English & Roman Urdu) hardware compatibility database built for smartphone repair technicians, micro-soldering engineers, and spare-parts wholesalers across Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and global repair markets.

Our platform indexes over 8,000+ smartphone models across 23 precision hardware categories—including Universal LCD Display Assemblies, Waterdrop Cut vs. No-Cut Tempered Glasses, OCA Optical Adhesive Sheets, OEM Battery Part Codes (BLP, BN/BM, EB-B), Charging Sub-Boards, BGA Power Management ICs (PMICs), eMMC/UFS Memory Chips, Multimeter Diode-Mode Cold GR Values, and Kernel Panic Log Diagnostics.

🔬 Our 3-Tier Hardware Verification Methodology

  • 🟢 Verified Fit (High Confidence): Cross-checked against official OEM Service Pack part numbers (such as Samsung GH82/GH96, Xiaomi 56000...), factory FPC ribbon stencils (e.g., BL-KL65A35.60, 830-066718, SK67025A-B-BOE), and physical workshop bench testing.
  • 🟡 Conditional Fit: Indicates parts that share active display or connector geometry but require technician verification of middle-frame bezels, 4G vs. 5G antenna grounding points, or PCB revision codes prior to installation.
  • 🚫 Quarantined Market Poster Traps: Explicitly flags false compatibility charts circulated in wholesale markets (such as mixing flat IPS displays with curved OLED models or swapping incompatible 4G/5G charging sub-boards) to protect repair shops from financial loss.
